## Research tab — Editor brief (copy everything below this line)

**Visual direction for hero image:** Simple operations desk: laptop showing a chronological list of customer messages with name/subject columns; coffee cup, notebook, small retail props. Documentary photo style, not SaaS marketing.

**Editor brief:**

Describe the **contact-lead workflow** in abstract terms only:

1. Visitor submits a form on the storefront.
2. A durable record is created with timestamp and channel (web, social, etc.).
3. The team receives an alert on a monitored inbox.
4. Owner and support partner can review the same queue later.

Emphasise **auditability**: who submitted, when, and from which channel. Email is a **backup alert**, not the system of record.

Include a short vignette: a honey seller, vintage dealer, or repair shop receiving a wholesale enquiry on Saturday and finding it waiting in one place on Monday.

**Do not name:** FormSubmission, endpoints, portal modules, or any internal product.

Neutral news tone. POPIA-aware: only store what the customer volunteered on the form.

**Do not mention:** APIs, headers, multi-tenant architecture, or how the software was built.
