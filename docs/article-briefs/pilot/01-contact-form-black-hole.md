## Research tab — Editor brief (copy everything below this line)

**Visual direction for hero image:** Split scene: left, a simple “Contact us” form on a phone screen; right, an empty inbox and an unanswered notification badge. Editorial, muted colours, no logos, South African small-shop feel (high street, not corporate).

**Editor brief:**

Frame as a **systems problem**, not a product launch. Explain that many independent retailers treat the contact form as “done” once it renders on the page, but owners often lack a single place to see, track, and act on messages.

Contrast email-only workflows (missed spam folders, shared inboxes, no audit trail) with storing enquiries in an operations hub tied to the business. Mention that **instant notification to a monitored address** plus **a dashboard both the owner and their support partner can see** reduces lost leads.

**Do not name any platform, product, or vendor.**

Local angle: traders, food producers, and service businesses that rely on WhatsApp and email today. Neutral Riverside Herald tone; cite public sources for any statistics about SME digital adoption or consumer trust.

**Do not mention:** APIs, databases, env configuration, specific software brands, or implementation steps.
