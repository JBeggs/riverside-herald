**Visual direction for hero image:** Main street row of independent shops with “Open” signs; one window shows a simple, working website on a phone held by a customer. Hopeful, grounded, non-Silicon Valley aesthetic.

**Editor brief:**

Opinion-leaning **infrastructure essay** for Riverside Herald readers.

Argue that investment in **repeatable local business tooling**—inventory, orders, contact handling, basic analytics—delivers more value for South African SMEs than chasing viral features or copycat apps.

Ground the piece in local reality: load-shedding, mobile-first shoppers, trust deficits online, and the cost of broken checkout or silent contact forms.

Celebrate unglamorous wins: form submissions that actually arrive, paid orders that reconcile, business hours and returns policies that match what happens in the shop.

Use anonymous case studies only (“a Western Cape food producer”, “a KZN vintage dealer”). No named clients or platforms.

**Do not mention:** monorepo, templates by name, crawlers, or any proprietary stack.

Cite public data where possible (SME digital adoption, e-commerce growth, consumer complaint trends) with full https references in the article body.
